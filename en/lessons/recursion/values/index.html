What row are you in?

You're at the movies and it's dark: you can't see your row number. You can't count the rows, but you can ask the person in front of you.

📜 How to know what row you are in

  1. If there is nobody in front: you are in row 1.
  2. If not: ask the person in front of you what row they are in…
  3. …and add 1 to what they tell you.

SCREEN

Each call returns its part

The factorial of 4 is 4 × 3 × 2 × 1. Look: that is 4 × the factorial of 3. Like at the movies, factorial(4) asks factorial(3) and uses its answer.

def factorial(n):    if n == 1:        return 1    else:        return n * factorial(n - 1)print(factorial(4))

Now you do the way back

The stack is already full: factorial(4) waits for factorial(3), which waits for factorial(2), which waits for factorial(1). Undo the stack: tap the card that can return and say what it returns.

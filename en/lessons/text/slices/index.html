Text in depth 2/5 · Slices

Cut out a piece

A slice takes a piece of the text. Drag the scissors (or tap them and use the arrow keys) and get “bow” and “rain” out of “rainbow”.

word = "rainbow"print(word[1:4])
  • "bow"
  • "rain"
word[1:4] → "ain"
Output

The end stays out

The numbers go on the cuts, not on the letters. What comes out in each case?

word = "rainbow"

  1. word[2:6]
  2. word[3:3]
  3. word[5:50]

From the start, to the end

Leave out the start and the slice begins at the beginning; leave out the end and it goes all the way to the end. Remove each one, one at a time.

word = "rainbow"print(word[0:4])
word[0:4] → "rain"
Output

The last letters

We want a file's extension: png, pdf… The names have different lengths. Which slice works for all three?

file = "notes.pdf"print(file[?])

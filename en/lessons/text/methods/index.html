Text machines

A method sticks to the text with a dot: text.upper(). It works like a machine: one text goes in and another comes out. Try all three.

.strip(): the spaces you can't see

When you type, an extra space sometimes sneaks in. .strip() removes the spaces at the edges. Try a name with spaces at the start or the end, and another with a space in the middle.

name = input("Name: ")clean = name.strip()print(f"[{clean}]")
